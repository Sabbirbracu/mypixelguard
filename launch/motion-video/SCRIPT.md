## Voice-over recording tips
- Record each scene as a separate take (`vo-01.wav` … `vo-10.wav`) or one long take — both work. Drop the files in `public/vo/` and tell me; I'll sync them to the scene start times above and duck the music under your voice.
- Leave ~0.3 s silence at the start of each take.
- Total speaking time is ~70 s of the 84 s, so there's room to breathe — don't rush.
