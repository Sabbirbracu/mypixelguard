# Voice-over — English recording script (line by line)

Record each line as a **separate file** named by take number: `vo-01.m4a` … `vo-17.m4a`.
Don't time it to the video. Speak at a natural pace and the lines get synced in the edit. If a line runs long, its scene is stretched to fit.

## Delivery
- **01–05 (the problem):** slower, lower, serious. Pause briefly before "Gone." and before "Not anymore."
- **06 onward (the solution):** brighter and confident. Smile while you talk; it comes through in the voice.
- **Tool names (08–14):** punch the first word ("Inspect —", "Clean —"), then a tiny pause.
- **17:** slow and clear. Land on "belong to you."
- **Pronunciation:** "MyPixelGuard" = *My-Pixel-Guard*, three clear parts. "EXIF" = *EE-ziff*.
